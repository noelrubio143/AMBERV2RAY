1Who we are

The App lets you connect to VPN / proxy servers (using protocols such as VLESS, VMess, Trojan, WebSocket and SSH) by loading a server configuration and, when you tap Connect, creating a tunnel. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at noelrubios1201@gmail.com.

3VPN service and your traffic

The App uses Android’s VpnService API. This is required for the App’s core function: to route your device’s network traffic through the server you selected. The VPN service is used only for that purpose.

  • The App itself does not read, store or analyze the content of your traffic or the websites and apps you use.
  • Your traffic is sent to the server in the protocol you selected and is then forwarded to its destination. The operator of that server can technically see connection metadata, such as your IP address, connection times and the destination addresses requested.
  • The VPN software on the servers operated for this App is set not to keep access logs. We do not keep a record of the websites or services you connect to through the VPN.
  • Like any server, the operating system may keep standard security and system records, such as the time and IP address of an SSH sign-in. These are used only to keep the server secure and working, are not used for any other purpose, and are deleted by the operating system’s automatic log rotation.
  • If you load a configuration that points to a server run by someone else, that server’s operator is responsible for its own data practices. Only connect to servers you trust.

4Advertising

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. These include banner ads, interstitial ads, app-open ads and rewarded ads (you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to add more connection time).

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to show you ads (including personalized ads, where allowed), to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud and to improve its services.

  • Learn how Google handles this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalization on your phone: Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path can vary by device).
  • If you watch a rewarded ad to the end, the App adds 2 hours to your time balance. Watching ads is always optional.

7App permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to servers, download configurations and load ads.
  • VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): to create the tunnel. Android shows you a system prompt before the VPN starts.
  • Foreground service and notifications: to keep the connection alive and show a notification while connected.
  • Wake lock: to keep the connection stable while the screen is off.
  • Wi-Fi state: for the optional hotspot sharing feature.
  • Vibrate: for notification feedback.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve and measure ads.

8Retention and deletion

  • On your device: credentials, settings, configuration and time balance stay until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Doing so deletes all of it and resets your time balance.
  • On our servers: the VPN software keeps no access logs. Standard operating-system security records are removed by automatic log rotation.
  • Advertising data: held by Google under its own policy.

To ask us to delete server-side data linked to your account identifier, email noelrubios1201@gmail.com.
