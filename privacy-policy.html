Privacy Policy

App: AMBER V2RAY (package: v2ray.noel120.dpdns.org)
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AMBER V2RAY app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. What the App does

AMBER V2RAY is a VPN / proxy client. It uses Android's VpnService to create a local VPN interface and route your device's internet traffic through the SSH or Xray server profile you select. The VPN is started only when you tap Connect.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not log, record, inspect, sell, or share your browsing activity, visited sites, DNS queries, or the content of your traffic.
  • We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos, location, or microphone/camera data.
  • The App does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores locally, in its private app storage: your server profiles (host, port, username, password/UUID, payload or SNI settings), your selected profile, app settings, and the last configuration update time. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

4. Network requests the App makes

  • Configuration updates: the App downloads a configuration file (server list and settings) from a GitHub-hosted repository (api.github.com / raw.githubusercontent.com). Standard connection data such as your IP address is visible to GitHub under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Subscription / account service: if you use a subscription or account feature, the App communicates with our server (ambersshvpn.vercel.app) to check your account or subscription status. This may include the account identifier or username you enter.
  • Your VPN server: your traffic is sent to the server profile you choose. The operator of that server can see the connection metadata that any VPN/proxy server sees. Only connect to servers you trust.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE – to connect and detect network changes.
  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE / VpnService – to create the VPN tunnel (Android shows its own VPN consent dialog).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK – to keep the connection running and show connection status.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to restore the service after restart where you enabled it.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE – used only to detect mobile network state/carrier type for connection behavior. It is not used to read or transmit your phone number or identifiers.
  • VIBRATE – for notification feedback.

6. Payments

If paid features are offered, payments are handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card or e-wallet details.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except as described above (GitHub for configuration downloads, your chosen server, and the payment provider if you pay), or when required by law.

8. Security

Traffic between the App and your server is carried through the SSH/Xray protocol you configure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

You can disconnect the VPN at any time, revoke the VPN permission in Android settings, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. To request deletion of any account data held on our server, contact us below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
